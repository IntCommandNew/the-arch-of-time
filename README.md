# The Arch of Time

**"Academia Mihăileană" in Augmented Reality**

An Android AR application that brings back the "Academia Mihăileană", the forerunner of the Alexandru Ioan Cuza University of Iași, at full scale, on the exact spot where it once stood. Walk around the lost academy, travel through four key moments of its history, and listen to an AI narrator who tells its story and answers your questions based on curated historical sources.

## About the project

The "Academia Mihăileană" was inaugurated in Iași in 1835 and is considered the forerunner of today's Alexandru Ioan Cuza University. Its buildings no longer exist: the stone arch that connected its two buildings was demolished in 1890, and the main building in 1963. Today there are streets and apartment buildings where the academy once stood.

The Arch of Time uses geospatial AR to show the academy where it stood, so users can see it from all sides by walking around it in the real world.

### Planned features

- **Geospatial placement**: the academy appears at its original location and real scale in Iași.
- **Free placement mode**: place, scale and rotate the model anywhere (table, park, classroom), then walk around it, for users who are not in Iași or for safer testing.
- **Time journey** through four moments:
  - **1835**: the academy is founded
  - **1845**: the arch connecting the two buildings is completed
  - **1890**: the arch is demolished
  - **1963**: the academy is demolished
- **AI narrator**: guides the user through the time journey and answers questions only from a curated historical knowledge source, so answers stay historically accurate.
- **Languages**: English by default, with Romanian support. Other languages are planned to be supported.
