## In PowerPoint

Everything works as it is: the three videos and the eight voice recordings are embedded in the file. Each one
plays when you click it (the clicker still moves to the next slide). There are no slide transitions and no
animations. The fonts (Bodoni Moda, EB Garamond, Open Sans, PT Sans Narrow) are embedded. The slides have no speaker notes.

## In Google Slides

Upload the .pptx to Google Drive and open it with Google Slides (the file is under Google's 100 MB import
limit). The text, layout, pictures and fonts come through. **Embedded video and audio do not:** Google Slides
keeps only a picture where each video was and drops the recordings. Put them back from Drive:

1. Upload the files of the `media/` folder to Google Drive.
2. For each row below: on that slide, select the picture or play button it replaces, **Insert > Video**
   (Google Drive tab) or **Insert > Audio**, pick the file, then drag and size it to the same place and delete
   the old picture.
3. For the voices, in **Format options > Audio playback**, choose "Play on click".
