## Facts used

- August 1834: Mihail Sturdza becomes prince; schools and the academy are founded soon after (author).
- 6 September 1834: the Epitropia Învățăturilor Publice (with Metropolitan Veniamin and Gheorghe Asachi) finds the
  Trei Ierarhi buildings too small and proposes the houses of Petrache Cazimir, next to Biserica Tălpălari (author).
- 20 November 1834: the Philosophy course opens at Trei Ierarhi (author).
- October 1834: the Cazimir houses are bought; 10 December 1834: contract with architect Gustav Freiwald (author).
- 16 June 1835: the academy's own building is inaugurated (author; the plaque quoted on ro Wikipedia).
- Inside: professors' apartments, dormitories for boarders, study rooms and classrooms, the chancellery, the
  archive, the dining hall and the infirmary (author).
- June 1837: the Voinescu houses across the street are bought (site of today's Colegiul Național); in 1838 the
  boarding school, stores, kitchen and administration move there; courses, library and the physics, mathematics
  and natural history cabinets stay in the Cazimir houses (author).
- The arch: proposed by Asachi to join the two buildings; plan and estimate by architecture professor
  A. Costinescu with the engineering students; foundation stone 19 October 1841; carved stone; finished
  September 1845; final reception 26 October 1847, without the planned inscriptions and bas-reliefs (author).
- 1845-1846: the gymnasium classes and the faculties move to the Voinescu houses; 120 boarders in the Cazimir
  houses (author).
- 1890: on the initiative of V. Paladi, director of the Liceul Național, the boarding house and the arch are
  demolished to build the high school's current building (author); also to widen the street (ro Wikipedia).
- July 1963: the main building, with its dome, is demolished for public utility (author).
- The academy's chapel was the neighbouring Biserica Tălpălari (en Wikipedia; ro Wikipedia, Casele Cazimir).
- Trei Ierarhi: built by Vasile Lupu, consecrated 1639; carved stone decoration "like lace" over all four facades;
  the Vasilian school in its courtyard; Asachi's Vasilian Gymnasium (1828) grew from the school at the monastery
  (ro Wikipedia).
- Colegiul Național: built 1890-1894 (architect Nicolae Gabrielescu) on the Voinescu houses' site; it calls itself
  the descendant of the Vasilian Gymnasium and of the Academia Mihăileană (ro Wikipedia).
- A reduced-scale replica of the arch was built for the 1936 exhibition in Parcul Expoziției (architect
  Gh. Grumăzescu) and still stands (ro Wikipedia); the arch gave Strada Arcu its name (author; ro Wikipedia).
- 1860: the academy's higher courses become the University of Iași, inaugurated on 26 October 1860 in the presence
  of Prince Alexandru Ioan Cuza; its lower classes become the national college (en / ro Wikipedia).
- The university's main building (Palatul Universității) was built 1893-1897 to plans by Louis Blanc, on the site
  of the old National Theatre, and inaugurated in 1897 in the presence of King Carol I (ro Wikipedia).
- 15 May 1835: Sturdza approves the first military fire brigade of Iași (author).
- 24 November 1843: Mihail Kogălniceanu's opening lecture of the first course of national history, at the academy
  (author).

## Contradictions (kept out of the quizzes)

- Founding: 1834 (decision, Wikipedia en; "20 martie 1834" on ro Colegiul) versus 1835 (inauguration).
- Inauguration day: 16 June 1835 (author, plaque) versus 6 June 1835 (Wikipedia text).
- The arch's foundation stone: 19 October 1841 (author) versus October 1842 (ro Wikipedia, Arcul text).
- The arch's columns: Doric (author) versus Ionic (ro Wikipedia).
- The main building's demolition: July 1963 (author) versus 1964 (en Wikipedia, Talpalari Church).
