# Sound samples

The videos in the [weekly progress](../README.md#weekly-progress) have no sound. This page has samples of the voices you hear in the app instead: Prince Mihail Sturdza and the narrator, in English and Romanian.

The voices were recorded once with AI voices made with ElevenLabs and are played by the app, line by line, as each card opens (see [AI usage](AIUsage.md)). Each sample is joined from those recordings, with the app's short pause between a card's title and its text and a one-second pause where you would tap to go on. GitHub can't play a sound file on a page, so each sample is a short video of the screen you see while you hear it. The sound files themselves are in [docs/audio](audio).
